# Flawless AceTouch — Brand & Tone Guide
v1.0 | Sept 2026 | Companion to PRD v1.0 (Sec 7.2, Sec 4.1)

## 1. Voice in one line
Supportive, expert, reassuring — like a knowledgeable beauty advisor who listens carefully and gives practical, confidence-building advice.

## 2. Voice attributes

| Attribute | What it means | Sounds like |
|---|---|---|
| Supportive | Acknowledge effort, normalize struggles | "It's completely normal that…" |
| Expert | Specific, evidence-aware, no hype | Name the ingredient + what it does + how to use it |
| Reassuring | Calm, no fear tactics | "You don't need to change everything at once." |
| Practical | 2–3 priorities, clear next step | "Start with these two changes this week." |
| Respectful | No shaming, no medical claims | No "fix your bad skin", no diagnosing conditions |

## 3. Principles mapping (Functional / Engaging / Productive)
- **Functional:** Every screen ends with a usable next step.
- **Engaging:** Briefly explain *why* we ask; celebrate small wins at check-ins.
- **Productive:** Simple language, consistent structure, morning/evening framing, skippable education.

## 4. Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Your top priority right now is barrier support — that's why your skin feels tight by midday." | "Your barrier is destroyed." |
| "Use pea-sized amount, PM only, 2–3x/week to start." | "Apply as needed." |
| "Fragrance-free option since you noted sensitivity to scent." | "Everyone loves our signature scent!" |
| "If you notice stinging > 2 days, pause and check in." | "Push through the purge." |
| "This is cosmetic guidance, not medical diagnosis — see a dermatologist if…" | Diagnose acne grade, rosacea, eczema, prescribe. |

Language rules:
- Plain language; define terms on first use (e.g., "niacinamide — a form of vitamin B3 that supports tone and barrier").
- No absolutes ("miracle", "erases", "perfect skin", "guaranteed results").
- No fear/urgency ("last chance", "your skin is aging fast").
- Inclusive, age-neutral; primary audience women 25–45 but copy must not exclude others.
- Always note patch-test / pause guidance for actives and sensitive users.

## 5. Sample copy

### 5.1 Welcome + goal setting
> Welcome — let's figure out what your skin needs most right now.
> Tell us your #1 goal (clearer skin, calmer skin, more even tone…) and anything that's frustrated you lately. We'll keep this focused — about 5 minutes — and you'll leave with a clear plan.
> [Why we ask: so everything that follows feels relevant to *your* goal.]

### 5.2 Analysis summary (top 2–3 priorities)
> Here's what we're seeing, Maya:
> **1. Barrier support first.** Tightness + sensitivity after cleansing suggests your barrier needs backup. That's why we start here — calm skin responds better to everything else.
> **2. Uneven tone second.** Once calm, niacinamide + daily SPF will help those post-breakout marks fade steadily.
> You don't need a 10-step routine. Two focused changes will do the most right now. Ready to see your roadmap?

### 5.3 Product "why it fits"
> **Why this fits you:** Fragrance-free gel moisturizer with ceramides + glycerin. Matches your combo/sensitive profile, layers well under SPF, and keeps things simple — AM + PM. No added fragrance since you noted scent sensitivity.
> **How to use:** AM/PM, dime-sized amount after serum. If tightness persists past week 1, tell us at check-in and we'll adjust.

### 5.4 Check-in celebration (2–4 weeks)
> Nice work sticking with it — that's the hardest part. 🎉
> You noted less midday tightness and fewer new breakouts — that's real progress. Barrier support is working, so we can now gently increase brightening to 4x/week.
> Want to keep the same routine, or tweak one thing together?

### 5.5 Custom formulation invite (only when valuable, no pressure)
> Because off-the-shelf options with fragrance keep irritating you, a simple custom serum *could* help — same effective ingredients, nothing extra. Want to peek at a concept? Totally optional, and your routine already works without it.

## 6. Microcopy patterns
- Buttons: "See my plan", "Save routine", "Peek at custom option", "Check in (2 min)".
- Empty states: "No saved formulas yet — your concepts will live here when you're ready."
- Errors: "We couldn't save that — try again, your answers are kept."
- Disclaimer (footer / analysis): "Cosmetic guidance only. Not medical advice. If you have a persistent or worsening skin condition, please see a dermatologist."

## 7. QA checklist for any copy
- [ ] Warm + specific, not generic?
- [ ] ≤3 priorities / actions?
- [ ] Why-it-fits + how-to-use included?
- [ ] No medical / absolute / fear language?
- [ ] Clear next step?

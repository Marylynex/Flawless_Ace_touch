# Pricing & Business Model — Flawless AceTouch

## Options Considered
1. **Free consult + affiliate:** $0 consult, earn 8–15% on recommended OTC products.
2. **Paid deep consult:** one-time $19–29 for full 6-step + routine PDF + 1 check-in.
3. **Custom formula premium:** one-time $45–85 per custom serum/moisturizer (made-to-order partner).
4. **Subscription check-ins:** $8–12/mo or $79/yr for quarterly re-consults, routine tuning, education.

## Recommended V1 Model (hybrid)
- **Free core consult + affiliate** (acquisition + trust) + **optional custom formula ($59 anchor)** + **optional check-in Plus ($9/mo, first check-in free).**
- Why: free consult maximizes completion (PRD goal: clarity + retention); custom carries margin; subscription only after value is proven at first free check-in. Avoid paywalling the core loop in V1.

## Unit Economics Sketch (per 100 consults, illustrative)
- Completion: 60 → 60 routines delivered.
- Affiliate: 60 × 35% buy rate × $55 AOV × 10% commission = **~$116**.
- Custom: 60 × 12% take rate × $59 × 45% gross margin = **~$191**.
- Plus: 60 × 30% free check-in return × 20% convert × $9/mo × 3 mo avg = **~$97**.
- Revenue/100 consults ≈ **$404 (~$4.04 per consult)**; COGS = formula fulfillment + affiliate ops.
- Levers: lift buy rate (why-fit + layering clarity), custom take rate (only-when-valuable targeting), check-in return (reminders).

## Next Steps
- V1: launch free + affiliate + $59 custom intent (manual fulfillment pilot); track PRD §8 metrics.
- V1.1: add paid deep consult ($24) as upsell for complex cases; introduce Plus after proving free check-in value.
- Kill/keep gates: custom take rate <5% → rework targeting; affiliate buy rate <20% → fix matching/assortment.
